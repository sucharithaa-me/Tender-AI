# Tender AI Research

## What is a Tender?

In business, a tender is a formal invitation to submit a competitive bid for a project, the actual offer submitted by a supplier, and a transparent way to control costs

## Why are Tenders Issued?

- Government procurement
- Private contracts
- Construction projects
- IT services

## Common Sections

- Tender ID
- Scope of Work
- Eligibility Criteria
- Submission Deadline
- Payment Terms
- Penalties
- Technical Requirements

## Problems Contractors Face

- Long documents
- Difficult legal language
- Missing deadlines
- Eligibility confusion

## Proposed Solution

Tender AI will:
- Summarize tenders
- Extract deadlines
- Identify risks
- Answer user questions
- Generate reports
